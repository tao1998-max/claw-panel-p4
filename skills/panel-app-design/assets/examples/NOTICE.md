# Example Asset Notice

## Fonts

`countdown/assets/countdown_cn.ttf` and `sd_fallback/app/assets/ui_cn.ttf` are
subsets of **Noto Serif SC** (Google / Adobe), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. The full license text is in `OFL.txt`.

The subsets contain only the characters used by the example UI. They are not
the full font and are not intended for reuse outside these examples.

## Background and sigil

`sd_fallback/sd_source/bg.jpg` and `sd_fallback/sd_source/sigil.png` are
original procedural artwork generated for this skill (Pillow), released for use
with this example. They do not contain third-party game art, logos, or
characters.

`sd_fallback/app/assets/sfx/click.ogg` is a short UI click from the project's
Blackjack demo and is included as a functional example.
